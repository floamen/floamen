THE EVERYDAY, RECONSIDERED

Fragrance,
designed for
the drive.

Floamen combines a reusable fragrance system with a minimal vent-integrated design. Fragrance oil is absorbed by lava stones inside the diffuser and gradually released through airflow.

Reusable. Refillable.
Purposefully designed.

A NATURAL FIT

Designed around the vent.
Not over it.

The open center keeps the original vent control visible and accessible, allowing the airflow to remain adjustable after installation.

Designed for selected compatible Mercedes-Benz vehicles with round turbine-style air vents.

REFILL. RECONNECT. GO.

Simple by design.
